# Update to 7.4.0-source-domains

1. Unzip `NKPI_SOURCE_Domains_v7_4_GitHub.zip`.
2. Upload the 99 extracted files to your existing repository root, replacing matching files and preserving the folder structure. Upload the contents, not the ZIP itself.
3. Keep `kcn_dual_calculator_app.py` as the Streamlit entry point. Requirements are unchanged.
4. Reboot Streamlit and reload the page. Check the footer for **7.4.0-source-domains**, model **3.1.0-interval-half**.
5. Import and verify the visits, then calculate as before.
6. At the bottom, click **SOURCE progression domains · Both eyes**. This replaces the previous three-measurement direction count in the same dropdown.
7. Each eye shows its 0–4 domain count, actual changes, threshold crossings and comparison-scope limitations. Open the measurement details for the structural 2-of-3 sensitivity check.

At least two domains crossing thresholds meets the numerical SOURCE criterion. It does not independently establish clinical progression or treatment need. Short follow-up, unknown quality/treatment history and other protocol restrictions remain visible. The prior-CXL numerical comparison is not validated for postoperative monitoring.

The NKPI model and score, half-weight scan interval, extraction, bilateral input/review, maps and projections are unchanged. There are no new required input fields. The domain summary is on-screen only; the existing PDF/JSON exports remain unchanged apart from the interface version label.

This is a complete package of 99 files. No patient images, imported ZIPs or private cohort records are included.
